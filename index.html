Science-driven biotechnology initiative

Turning molecular signals into earlier decisions.

Building the scientific foundations for the next generation of molecular intelligence in precision medicine.

Cellens is a science-driven biotechnology initiative focused on understanding how circulating molecular signals can support earlier clinical decisions and longitudinal disease monitoring through rigorous translational research.

Explore our research Contact us
Our vision

Biology continuously generates information.

Long before many diseases become clinically evident, molecular signals are already present in circulation.

We believe these signals represent one of the most valuable yet underused sources of clinical information. Cellens exists to build the scientific knowledge, analytical methods and translational framework required to interpret those signals responsibly.

Our approach

Evidence before claims

We do not believe better healthcare begins with more complex technologies. We believe it begins with better biological understanding.

Our work combines molecular biology, clinical medicine and translational research to investigate how circulating biomarkers may contribute to earlier and more informed medical decisions.

Scientific integrity

Every hypothesis must be challenged. Every result must be reproducible. Every conclusion must remain proportional to the available evidence.

Our objective is not to create expectations. Our objective is to generate evidence.

First research program

Colorectal cancer

Our current research explores whether circulating microRNA signatures may provide clinically meaningful information in two complementary contexts.

01

Early detection

Exploring whether specific molecular signatures may contribute to identifying advanced colorectal lesions before clinical progression.

02

Disease monitoring

Investigating whether longitudinal changes in circulating molecular signals may eventually contribute to monitoring disease evolution and treatment response.

At the present stage, these objectives remain scientific hypotheses under investigation and require extensive biological and clinical validation.
Technology

Three complementary pillars

Circulating molecular signals

Translational molecular research

Connecting laboratory discoveries with clinically relevant questions.

Analytical frameworks

Developing rigorous methodologies for interpreting molecular information obtained from biological samples.
